Hi!
My name is April Juarez Diaz. Growing up in the vibrant Mexican
culture exposed me to bold colors, intricate designs, and a deep
sense of pride in colorful self-expression. This and my passion
for art and cosmetics inspired me to pursue a career in nail
design. Nail artistry has allowed me to help people showcase
their unique personality and creativity right at their fingertips.
The rich cultural heritage that shaped me continues to influence
my work, as I strive to bring a little piece of that colorful
world into every nail design I create and help bring to life.
